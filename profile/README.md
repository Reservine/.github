# Reservine

**Reservations, payments, and smart access — one automated system.**

[Website](https://reservine.io/en) · [Documentation](https://docs.reservine.io/) · [Book a demo](https://reservine.io/en/contact)

</div>

Reservine helps self-service businesses run around the clock without a front
desk. Customers reserve and pay online, then receive time-limited access to the
space automatically.

## One platform for the whole customer journey

- **Online booking** — flexible availability, recurring slots, dynamic pricing,
  memberships, vouchers, and multi-branch management.
- **Payments and invoicing** — Stripe, Apple Pay, Google Pay, automated invoices,
  and operational reporting.
- **Smart access** — unique, time-limited PIN codes through Nuki and TTLock.
- **Customer communication** — configurable email and SMS confirmations,
  reminders, access instructions, and review requests.
- **Flexible integration** — hosted booking pages or an embeddable booking
  experience for an existing website.

Reservine is built in Czechia for self-service fitness centers, wellness and
sauna facilities, studios, coworking spaces, meeting rooms, and other spaces
that should be bookable 24/7.
